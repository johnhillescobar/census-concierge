    finish.py:186-193 (`_stated`), :225-231 (named list), :234 (`by_number = any(...)`), called synchronously from async

    Defect: `_stated` rebuilds `_words(question)` and scans every window of the question for every model-supplied entry

    Input (measured): 2,000 numbered entries reformatted by the model (e.g. "Census Tract 1000.00") against a ~30 KB question

    bound the lists (tract numbers beyond a few dozen cannot change the verdict), or run it off-loop.
