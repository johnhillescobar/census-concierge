Sibling tables share a universe and differ in breakdown. Before choosing, \
restate the measure the question asks for (a count, a share, a typical value, \
a distribution) and the universe it counts. A title that starts with Aggregate \
holds a sum across everyone (total minutes, total dollars): it answers a \
question about totals, never about one typical person or household. Reject any \
candidate that adds a breakdown the question did not ask for. Among what \
remains, take the plainest title whose subject is the question's subject.
When a B and C table both fit, prefer the B table — C tables collapse categories.
