Sibling tables share a universe and differ in breakdown. Prefer the table whose title covers the subject of the question most directly, including any breakdown the question names. Skip a title built around a summary statistic (Aggregate, Median, Mean, Share, Index) unless the question asks for that statistic. Aggregate means a sum across everyone, so it answers only totals.
When a B and C table both fit, prefer the B table — C tables collapse categories.
