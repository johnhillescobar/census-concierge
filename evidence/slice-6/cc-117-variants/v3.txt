Sibling tables share a universe and differ in breakdown. Before choosing, \
restate the measure the question asks for (a count, a share, a typical value, \
a distribution) and the universe it counts. Reject any candidate that adds a \
breakdown the question did not ask for, or whose title says Aggregate when the \
question wants counts or a typical value. Among what remains, take the plainest \
title whose subject is the question's subject.
When a B and C table both fit, prefer the B table — C tables collapse categories.
