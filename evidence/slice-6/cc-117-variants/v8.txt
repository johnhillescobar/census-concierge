Sibling tables share a universe and differ in breakdown. Take the candidate whose title names the subject of the question. A longer title that adds "by ..." is a cross-tab: take it only when the question also concerns what follows "by", otherwise take the plainer title. Also skip a title built around a summary statistic (Aggregate, Median, Mean, Share, Index) unless the question asks for that statistic. Aggregate means a sum across everyone, so it answers only totals.
When a B and C table both fit, prefer the B table — C tables collapse categories.
