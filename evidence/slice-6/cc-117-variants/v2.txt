Sibling tables share a universe and differ in what they measure. Read the title \
for the measure: "Aggregate" tables hold summed amounts, "Median" and "Mean" \
tables hold one typical value, other tables hold counts by category or band. \
Choose the table whose measure is what the question asks for: a question about \
how many or how much of a typical value wants counts or a typical value, not sums. \
Prefer the table that is only about the subject over one crossed with another \
variable the question never names.
When a B and C table both fit, prefer the B table — C tables collapse categories.
