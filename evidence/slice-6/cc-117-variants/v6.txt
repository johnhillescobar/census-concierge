Sibling tables share a universe and differ in breakdown. Prefer the plainest \
title: the one that is just the question's subject, with no "by ..." \
cross-tab and no summary statistic (Aggregate, Median, Mean, Share, Index) \
unless the question asks for that statistic or names that breakdown. A title \
that starts with Aggregate holds a sum across everyone (total minutes, total \
dollars) and answers only a question about totals.
When a B and C table both fit, prefer the B table — C tables collapse categories.
