  "Median household income in the past 12 months (in 2024 inflation-adjusted dollars) | 64000 (margin of error 900) | Harris County, Texas (0500000US48201) | acs5 2019"

and the model, told to state a figure "exactly as it appears there", reports 2019 dollars as 2024 dollars.
REPRODUCED (probe_dollars.py, real data/raw cache: _labels('acs5',2019,...) says "in 2019 inflation-adjusted

(strip_vintage folds the year out of labels), so the mismatch is not filtered upstream. judge_figures.py:75 builds its
FACTS labels the same way, so the instrument shares the blind spot and cannot see this error.
Suggested direction (not applied): look labels/title up per distinct row year among the shown figures.
