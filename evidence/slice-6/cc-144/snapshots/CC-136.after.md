Owner rationale and architectural direction (2026-11-13)

The overarching goal is to eliminate regex-based interpretation, validation and repair of user meaning, and replace it with more robust mechanisms. A newer model is an enabling choice, not the final architectural outcome. The owner's practical observation is that Googling questions and asking for the corresponding Census API shape often produces better results than the application's regex-heavy workflow. This is qualitative motivation for a controlled comparison, not a measured benchmark or a claim that the LLM is the only problem.

Target behavior: the model derives explicit structured intent from the question and grounded table/API metadata; deterministic code checks types, enums, required geography parents, valid identifiers, dataset/vintage availability, cell/universe consistency, variable limits and faithful execution. Missing or invalid intent receives bounded model repair with concrete structured feedback. No regex, keyword scan, substring heuristic or equivalent prose parser may supply or repair meaning. Removing regex must not remove correctness checks or recreate the same heuristics in another form.

Zero refers to semantic regex validation in the question-to-answer path. Separately inventory any remaining regex for purely syntactic identifiers/redaction or other nonsemantic uses and justify or replace it with ordinary parsing where appropriate; do not claim repository-wide zero regex from semantic isolation alone. Preserve security/redaction and explicit-field validation. The experiment must establish what evidence the model needs, not merely select the best prose generator.
