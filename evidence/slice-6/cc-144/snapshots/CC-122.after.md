Owner-approved audit tightening — 2026-11-13

Why this is important

Qualitative comparisons such as 'higher', 'largest' or 'increasing' are factual claims even without digits. Numerical rendering alone cannot establish them.

Acceptance criteria additions

AC1. Compute material comparative/trend/ranking claims from explicit typed operations and grounded operands, with source lineage, units, universe, periods and comparison scope. The model cannot assert a ranking over an unfetched population or a trend from one observation. Qualitative wording must be bound to the verified structured result; no regex or prose-number extraction is introduced.

AC2. Distinguish point-estimate ordering from statistical significance. Significance, 'unusually high', causal or other claims requiring an unsupported benchmark/method are omitted or explicitly identified as unsupported; MOE and overlap limitations remain visible. CC-131 owns claim selection and reconciliation; this story owns deterministic calculation support.

Test cases additions

TC1. Two estimates support a point comparison; one estimate cannot support a trend or 'highest among all' claim. A partial row preview cannot establish a full-population rank.

TC2. Equal values, sentinels, incompatible universes/units and overlapping periods yield defined limitations. Overlapping uncertainty does not become an unsupported significance claim; unsupported causal/benchmark statements are not emitted as established facts.
