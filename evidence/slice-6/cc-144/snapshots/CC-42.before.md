## Context

Follow-up quality must be measured over repeated runs, not assumed from one good example. This ticket builds the measurement and records a baseline BEFORE any resolution is built, so CC-43, CC-107 and CC-108 have something to beat. It runs first among the slice 6 stories, after the geography bugs (CC-103/104/105/114), so the baseline is not dominated by known wrong-level answers.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] `evals/multiturn.toml` holds at least 18 cases of 2-3 turns each: 6 geography swaps, 6 variable additions, 6 with an unresolvable reference (at 3 repeats one trial is 11 points on 9 trials, too coarse to compare). Each case names the expected final `table_id`, `geographies` (GEOIDs), `years` and geography level.

- [ ] At least 2 of the 6 swap cases start from a turn that names a non-place level (county or tract) and expect the final plan at that same level.

- [ ] Every case's expected values pass `scripts/verify_golden.py` against real metadata (no invented table IDs or GEOIDs).

- [ ] `scripts/run_demo.py` scores them with `--repeat 3` and reports `multiturn_answered_rate` separately from the single-turn rate; a case passes when the final plan is right in at least 2 of 3 repeats, and the trial-level rate is reported too. The single-turn rate is unchanged by this ticket.

- [ ] A case counts as passed only when the final plan matches the expected table, GEOIDs, years and geography level (the `for=` level of every leg, same check as the CC-114 scorer), not merely when the HTTP call succeeded.

- [ ] The baseline (no resolution built) is recorded in `evidence/slice-6/` with the run count, per-case results, p95, and the single-turn long_tail answered_rate measured in the same run.

- [ ] A floor at baseline minus 0.05 is proposed in the PR for the owner to commit; the agent does not edit `budgets.toml`.

- [ ] A deliberately broken resolver (always returns the first turn's plan) scores below the baseline on the swap cases, proving the cases can fail (mutation check).

- [ ] `make check` passes with `budgets.toml` unchanged.

## Test cases

- Happy: one geography-swap, one variable-addition, and one unresolved-reference case each score correctly across three repeats.

- Edge: case-level majority scoring treats two correct trials out of three as pass and one out of three as fail.

- Novel: metadata-generated tables and geographies absent from prompts and implementation literals are included without exposing sealed content.

- Regression: the single-turn demo scorer and its long_tail rate are unchanged by adding the multi-turn runner.

- Failure: a case with an invalid table, GEOID, year, or level is rejected by verify_golden before any scored run.
