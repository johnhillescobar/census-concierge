Owner-approved audit tightening — 2026-11-13

Why this is important

Valid structured requests and accurate digits can coexist with unsupported explanatory prose. Material claims and alternative interpretations must remain faithful to evidence.

Acceptance criteria additions

AC1. Represent material answer claims through grounded structured facts or supported typed operations before presenting them. Qualitative comparisons, ranks and trends require CC-122 evidence; categorical claims require authoritative metadata or fetched evidence. Use constrained rendering or another demonstrably grounded mechanism in the existing loop, not a new regex/prose parser or unmeasured runtime judge stack.

AC2. When material claims lack operands, benchmark, coverage or method, bounded reconciliation obtains the missing supported evidence or omits/qualifies the claim with an explicit limitation. Never invent a comparison, causality, significance or confidence guarantee. Assess false claims and unjustified withholding separately so silence cannot inflate correctness.

AC3. Alternative results distinguish a faithful answer from a related but different interpretation. Preserve the original requested meaning and disclose universe/scope/time changes; selecting an option must not retroactively turn an incorrect primary answer into a correct one.

Test cases additions

TC1. An answer containing correct digits but an unsupported 'highest', 'increasing' or 'unusually high' claim fails the claim oracle; wording without digits does not evade grounding.

TC2. Fully grounded comparisons survive rendering; missing benchmark, partial coverage, unsupported causality or significance produce a limitation rather than an invented fact. Test structured outputs/evidence, not exact prose strings.

TC3. A related alternative with a different universe or period is disclosed as a changed interpretation and cannot pass the original request's correctness oracle.
