Why this is important

Retrieval@10 measures table presence; users need inspectable and semantically appropriate cells and URLs to benefit from that presence.

Acceptance criteria additions

AC1. For offered alternatives expose grounded table identity, title, universe and available cell/definition metadata with dataset/vintage provenance. Distinguish a catalog candidate from an alternative validated for the current request. Do not imply every retrieved table answers the question.

AC2. Provide the metadata-backed cell/universe and availability checks consumed by CC-115. An alternative represented as executable must match requested measure, compatible units/denominator, geography and vintage, or explicitly disclose a changed interpretation requiring user selection. No empty-cell total fallback.

AC3. Report retrieved-table presence separately from offered-correct-alternative presence and executable semantic suitability. Freeze independent expected permissible alternatives; do not define correctness from whichever candidates the model happened to produce. Feed the CC-132 recovery instrument.

Test cases additions

TC1. A correct table retrieved in the raw top ten but omitted from the offered list counts as retrieval success and offered-alternative failure, not end-to-end recovery.

TC2. Sibling tables with different universes or breakdowns expose those differences; a superficially matching title cannot certify the wrong denominator/cells.

TC3. A historically available candidate and an incompatible-current-vintage candidate receive distinct provenance/validation states; selecting the former uses its actual supported cells without changing requested time.
