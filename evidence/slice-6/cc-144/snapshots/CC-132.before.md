Owner-approved audit tightening — 2026-11-13

Why this is important

Interpretation accuracy, primary-answer correctness and recovery via alternatives are distinct outcomes. Certification must not infer one from another or let aggregate preference conceal factual errors.

Existing AC/TC, earned floors, family-based uncertainty, repeat-3 comparisons, mutation checks, cold review, post-merge evidence and sealed/E2E transcript protections remain binding. These additions do not lower a threshold, authorize a budget increase, reopen a Done story or claim implementation is complete. Refresh affected closure matrices before shipping.

Acceptance criteria additions

AC1. Publish separate metrics for joint interpretation, primary end-to-end semantic answer correctness, raw retrieval@10, correct-alternative offered rate, conditional alternative recovery and overall assisted-answer success. Define each numerator/denominator before results. Existing 98% interpretation targets are not relabeled as 98% final-answer success. Preserve all existing absolute quality floors and B/P/A/L non-regression gates.

AC2. Freeze a supported recoverable cohort whose correct permissible alternatives are independently labeled. Conditional recovery is successful correct-answer selections divided by all planned selections of a correct offered alternative after a failed primary answer; failed requests/timeouts stay in the denominator. Also report failures where no correct alternative was offered. Assisted success requires a correct primary answer or an actually executed correct alternative, not table presence or URL validity alone. Report cases/trials and family-clustered intervals separately; zero eligible cases means not applicable with counts, not 100%.

AC3. Before evaluating implementation, freeze numerical acceptance targets and sampling/stop rules for the new alternative and claim metrics, with an explicit owner-approved metric amendment. At least 30 independent families for owned stochastic cohorts, repeat-3 initially and all deterministic fixtures passing; retain stronger final-suite requirements. Until those targets and sufficient evidence exist, the new criteria are unverified. No arbitrary target is inferred from retrieval@10 or invented after results.

AC4. Certification includes CC-115 alternative-selection semantics, CC-130 candidate validity and CC-122/131 qualitative-claim grounding. Report material unsupported-claim rates and answer coverage; zero silent substitution/fabrication remains required in the finite acceptance suite. A model judge is supplemental and cannot override factual or metadata failures.

Test cases additions

TC1. Correct interpretation with wrong executed cells or unsupported prose is not a correct primary answer; a 98% interpretation score cannot satisfy the separate answer metric.

TC2. Wrong primary/correct raw top-ten table/not offered yields no recovery. Wrong primary/correct offered alternative/failed execution also yields no recovery. Correct executed alternative yields assisted success while the primary remains a failure.

TC3. No eligible recovery cases, timed-out selections and repeated paraphrases preserve honest denominators and uncertainty. Easy cases cannot replace missing independent families or category coverage.

TC4. Missing frozen thresholds, unsupported material claims, stale evidence or failed alternative semantics prevents certification even when HTTP validity and judge preference are high.
