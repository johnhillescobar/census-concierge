Retained scope and acceptance criteria (read with the binding alignment above)
## Context

A follow-up that cannot be resolved gets options and a warning, never a blocking question. Users who do not know the ecosystem cannot answer one.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] For a follow-up with no resolvable referent (for example "and the other one" after a result with one geography), the response is 200 with a warning whose `code` is `unresolved_reference`, a one-sentence `detail`, and up to 3 `candidates` (the existing `GeoSpec` shape) ranked most likely first.

- [ ] The response still renders the full Census URL for the best candidate, including when its fetch fails. The best candidate's result carries MOE, GEOID and universe, and the response lists alternative tables.

- [ ] The response contains no field that asks the user a question they must answer before getting a result (checked on structured fields, not prose).

- [ ] Choosing a candidate through the plan strip sends a normal plan override and returns a normal answer with MOE, GEOID and universe.

- [ ] The 6 unresolvable cases in the CC-42 set pass at `--repeat 3` at a case-level rate recorded in the PR (a case passes when right in at least 2 of 3 repeats).

- [ ] Existing warning codes and `ambiguous_place` behavior are unchanged (existing tests pass unmodified).

- [ ] `make demo --repeat 3` single-turn long_tail answered_rate, at the same `--repeat` and the geography-level scorer, is no more than 3 points below the 0.742 baseline (>= 0.712) and no more than 3 points below this ticket's own pre-run; both runs are reported. Same-code runs vary by about 2 points (0.725 vs 0.742), so one run is not a verdict.

- [ ] `make check` passes with `budgets.toml` unchanged.

## Generalization criteria (added 2026-10-01, see CC-3 AC6-AC11)

- Replaces "a case-level rate recorded in the PR": at least 5 of 6 regression unresolvable cases pass, and the sealed unresolvable rate is within 10 points of it.

- False positives: `unresolved_reference` fires on at most 1 of the 36 regression swap and addition trials, at most 1 of the sealed resolvable trials, and never on a single-turn question. Warning on everything must fail this ticket.

## Test cases

- Happy: an unresolved follow-up returns HTTP 200, unresolved_reference, ranked candidates, and executes the best candidate with URL, MOE, GEOID, universe, and alternatives.

- Edge: the prior turn exposes only one plausible referent; the response still returns a result and warning rather than asking a blocking question.

- Novel: a prior plan built from metadata-only geographies not named in prompts or visible examples produces at most three deterministically ranked candidates.

- Regression: existing ambiguous_place and other warning behavior remains unchanged.

- Failure: no structured field requires a user reply before a result, and an invalid selected candidate fails safely through normal plan validation.
