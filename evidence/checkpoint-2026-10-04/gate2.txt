   - Input: `PINS=[(r"\bcell phones\b","B28")]` followed by `re.search(p, q)` is never flagged. The same goes for f-string patterns, because the Constant parts of a JoinedStr are not the arg, and for `pattern=` keyword args.

   - Fix: add all `n.keywords` values to `in_regex`, and descend into JoinedStr children. Better, apply `regex2` to every string constant in any module that calls `re`.

3. LOW - check_budgets.py:267. When `demo` exists but has no numeric `p95_latency_seconds`, `_p95_check` falls through to the stale top-level key.

   - Fix: take the `elif` branch only when `demo` is not a dict.

4. LOW - check_invariants.py:511, 405 and 133 (the `_regex_calls` caller `_regex_counts_now`).
