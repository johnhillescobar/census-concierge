========== Gate 2 Pass 1 -- generic bugs / silent-wrong-answer ==========
cold read of origin/main..HEAD plus unstaged api/src and tests; no
implementer chat. Confirmed two behaviours with a local import (not eval/demo).

Pass 1 findings:

1. split_versus fires on every "versus" / "compared to" / "vs" / leading
   "compare … to", with no check that both sides are places. ResolveGeographyTool
   then requires each side to produce specs or the whole call returns that
   side's empty/fail result and never sets compare.
   Golden q18 "Renter versus owner households in Milwaukee" split to
   ('Renter', 'owner households in Milwaukee') and resolved to compare=False,
   legal=True, 0 specs, detail "no place matched 'Renter'". Milwaukee is not
   looked up. The versus tests pass the full question into resolve_geography;
   that is the calling convention the feature needs in order to see "versus".
   "population of Denver compared to 2017" splits to ('population of Denver',
   '2017'); a year on the right aborts the resolve. Slice 3 named both
   "since 2017" and "compared to" as standard questions; the splitter cannot
   tell a year from a parent geography.

2. Unstaged _response_geoid returns "" whenever len(rows) > 1. That stops a
   two-area comparison from being labelled with geographies[0].geoid (the
   intended fix), but it also blanks a one-geography year series. Assemble of
   two Harris County year-rows with fallback 0500000US48201 yields geoid ''.
   Contract text: empty when many areas are returned, not when many rows are.
   test_many_rows_do_not_name_one_geoid was changed to _harris() plus two
   different county GEO_IDs; it does not distinguish many areas from many
   vintages of one area.
