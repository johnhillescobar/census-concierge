## Pass A — generic bugs (diff only, no intent)

Cold-read of origin/main...HEAD. No product intent.

Control flow
- apply_variable_plan still filters plan.attempted through drop_incompatible,
  then only relabels years already omitted as unpublished_vintage.
  Incompatible attempted years are dropped, not joined.
- ACS1 204/404 fallback rebuilds a fresh ACS5 plan and runs apply_variable_plan
  again. First-plan classifications are not reused (no stale attempted set).
- question_years is concatenated onto the tool years (unique_years keeps
  first-seen order), then is_series / span_years may densify. The model's
  years= list is not the final requested set when question_years is wired.
  That is a control-flow change, not lost omitted/reasons state.

Lost state
- omitted and reasons stay lockstep in apply_variable_plan (copy, rewrite
  extra as variable_not_in_vintage, extend dropped). _pack stores both.
  _tool_content zips them strict=True.
- variable_not_in_vintage at assemble no longer scans table_facts / question
  text. It only reads fetch.omitted_years / omission_reasons. Years not
  recorded on the artifact are not warned. Producer tests cover the record
  path; a missing fetch artifact yields no warning (also no joined rows).

Incorrect reasons
- introduction_omissions / _published_hole: a published hole on any dataset
  in the requested span reclassifies unpublished years before this dataset's
  first in-span presence as variable_not_in_vintage. A hole on ACS1 can
  relabel ACS5 unpublished years (and the reverse). Years stay omitted; the
  reason can be the wrong code.
- vintage_gap_2020 is not in the unpublished list used for extra, so 2020
  stays vintage_gap_2020.

Silent joins
- drop_incompatible now requires matching M when the reference vintage lists
  any M. estimate_table() returns E-only suffixes; _required_suffixes re-pairs
  M from the reference. Missing-M years are omitted, not fetched.
- If the reference vintage lists no M, M is not required (E-only years stay).
  That is a join of years the URL asked M for, if the matrix omitted margins.
- lookup is None → apply_variable_plan is a no-op (no variable filter).
  Production default_tools passes table_facts.

Budget / LOC
- Nothing in the diff raises a budget. Machines in pass B.

Pass A findings:
- introduction_omissions can attach variable_not_in_vintage using a hole on
  a different dataset (incorrect reason; not a silent join).
- variable_not_in_vintage zips omitted/reasons with strict=False; a length
  mismatch drops years from the warning without error.
- nothing else: no lost omitted/reasons on the producer path, no attempted
  year kept after drop_incompatible failure.
